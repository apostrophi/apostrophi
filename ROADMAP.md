# Roadmap

What's planned for Apostrophi. Items have no dates, and the order isn't a commitment. To suggest something, [open an issue](https://github.com/apostrophi/apostrophi/issues).

- **Windows and Linux.** Apostrophi runs on macOS 13 or later today. Builds for Windows and Linux come next.
- **Chrome Web Store.** Install the extension from the store, without Developer mode or Load unpacked.
- **Firefox and Safari.** The extension works in Chrome, Arc, Brave, and Edge. Firefox and Safari versions follow.
- **More sources.** Sync saves from Bluesky, Tumblr, and Dribbble alongside the [current sources](docs/supported-sources.md).
- **iPhone and iPad.** Browse your library and save to it from your phone or tablet.
- **Import from more apps.** Eagle libraries import today with their folders. Bookmark managers such as Raindrop.io come next.
- **Region notes.** Pin a note to part of an image or a moment in a video.
- **Plugins.** Extend Apostrophi with JavaScript plugins that run on your Mac.
- **Assistant with your MCP servers.** Give Assistant tools from MCP servers you run, the way Cursor and Claude already reach your library through Apostrophi.
